# TechTonic Store — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
**Brand Name:** TechTonic Store  
**Slogan:** *"Engineered for the Modern Digital Lifestyle"*  
**Market Segment:** B2C Modern Consumer Electronics  
**Location:** Toul Kork Flagship Experience Center (#271 Monireth Blvd, Phnom Penh, Cambodia)  

TechTonic Store provides modern consumers, creators, software engineers, and digital nomads with high-precision electronics, acoustic hardware, and ergonomic workspace gear.

---

## 2. Product Categories
1. **Audio & Acoustics:** Studio reference ANC headphones, lossless TWS earbuds, beryllium dynamic drivers.
2. **Smart Wearables:** AMOLED fitness watches, biosensor trackers, titanium smart rings.
3. **Workspace Peripherals:** 75% mechanical keyboards, custom silent tactile switches, ergonomic vertical mice, CNC aluminum stands.
4. **Power & Mobile:** 100W Gallium Nitride (GaN III) fast chargers, MagSafe wireless battery packs, braided silicone cables.

---

## 3. Target Audience
- **Software Engineers & Coders:** Demanding low-latency input devices, high-wattage single-cable docking, and noise-cancelling acoustic isolation.
- **University Students & Researchers:** Requiring all-day battery life, lightweight mobility, and value-engineered pricing.
- **Content Creators & Remote Professionals:** Seeking studio microphones, reference monitoring, and ergonomic comfort for 8+ hour screen sessions.

---

## 4. Key Value Propositions
- **Same-Day Express Dispatch:** Free delivery in Phnom Penh for orders over $100 within 3 hours via GrabExpress.
- **1-Year Serialized Warranty:** Genuine manufacturer guarantee supported by our local Phnom Penh service center.
- **Cambodia National Bank (NBC) Bakong KHQR:** Native QR scan support for ABA Mobile, Wing Bank, ACLEDA, and Sathapana.
- **30-Day Hassle-Free Exchange:** Confidence in fit and switch feel.
